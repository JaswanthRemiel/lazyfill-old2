What lazyfill is

lazyfill is a Chrome extension (and a small web page) that fills in job applications from a profile you keep, tracks the applications you send, and can write draft answers with an AI service you connect. This policy says what it stores, where it goes, and how to delete it.

What lazyfill reads on web pages

lazyfill runs on the pages you visit so it can notice a job application form and fill it. It reads form fields, their labels, and (to name the job) the page title, company and description. This happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to me for pages that are not job applications. On a page it recognises as a job application, the job title, company, address and description are kept in your browser for the session and saved to your tracker when you autofill or submit.

AI features (optional)

If you add a Gemini or OpenAI API key, then only when you use an AI feature your browser sends the following straight to that provider (not to me): for Import with AI, images of your resume pages, their text and their links; for Generate with lazyfill, the question you selected, a summary of your profile (your contact details are left out), and the job’s title, company and description. The provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Free-tier plans of some providers, Google’s Gemini API among them, may use what you send to improve their products; paid plans generally do not. Please read your provider’s terms. If you never add a key, nothing is sent to any AI service.

Who can see your data

  • You. Firestore rules allow only your signed-in, email-verified account to read or write your data.
  • Google, as the host of Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, acting as my processor. Servers may be outside your country.
  • Your AI provider, only for the requests described above.
  • Me, technically: as the owner of the Firebase project I can open stored data in the Firebase console. I will not read your data except to keep the service running, fix a problem you report, protect against abuse or security issues, or comply with the law.

I do not sell your data, rent it, use it for advertising, use it for credit or lending decisions, or share it with anyone else. There are no analytics, trackers or ads in the extension or on the web page.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept until you delete it.
  • Delete everything: in the extension, open the dashboard, then Settings, then Delete my account. This deletes your cloud data and your account, and clears the extension’s data in your browser. Copies in Google’s backups are removed on Google’s schedule.
  • Take a copy first: the Applications page exports CSV and JSON, and Profiles exports JSON.
  • Removing the extension clears what it holds in your browser but does not delete your cloud account; use Delete my account first.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict the use of your data, and to complain to a regulator. Most of this you can do yourself in the app as above; for anything else open an issue on the project’s GitHub page and ask for a private way to reach me, without including personal data in the issue. Where the GDPR applies, my legal basis is providing the service you asked for (contract) and, for the optional AI features, your choice to use them (consent), which you can withdraw by not using them or removing your key.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Access is limited by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules. Email verification is required before any data is saved. No system is perfectly secure; keep your password private and your AI key secret.

Children

lazyfill is not directed at children and is not for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, I will update the date above and, for significant changes, say so in the extension.
